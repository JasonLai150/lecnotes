2. `src/lecnotes/mdparse.py`:
   - `new_parser()` now chains `.use(dollarmath_plugin, allow_labels=False,
     allow_space=False, allow_digits=False, double_inline=True)` onto the existing

     `math_inline_double` contribute their `.content`; `image` recurses into its
     children; `softbreak`/`hardbreak` contribute a space; everything else
     contributes nothing.
